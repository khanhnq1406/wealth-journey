## Overview

Users need to track currency-based investment assets (e.g. USD, EUR) within their portfolio, with real-time or cached current prices displayed — similar to how gold and silver investments show current asset prices. This feature connects FOREIGN_CURRENCY investments to the existing admin-configured asset display price system instead of treating them as fully custom (manual) assets.
